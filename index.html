🕒 Our new sleep & meal rhythm

7:30 AM wake → 8:30 PM last snack → 9:30 PM sleep.
With breakfast around 7:30–8:00 AM the next morning, the overnight food gap is about 11–11½ hours. The hour between his evening snack and sleep also gives him time to settle after eating.

💬 Little language moments

You don't have to make the whole day speech practice. Try three relaxed 10–15 minute periods of face-to-face play. Follow his interests, use short phrases, then pause so he has a chance to communicate with a look, gesture, sound, sign, point or word attempt.

Easy car game: “Car. Go!” → push the car → “Stop!” → “Go!” → pause → “More?” → wait for any communication attempt → “More car!”

🍓 Easy meal ideas

🌞 Breakfast

  • Oatmeal + mashed banana
  • Scrambled egg + soft fruit
  • Greek yogurt + mashed berries
  • Toast strips + avocado
  • Mini pancakes + fruit

🥪 Lunch

  • Mac & cheese + soft peas
  • Avocado + soft scrambled egg
  • Soft pasta + tomato sauce
  • Mashed beans + avocado
  • Grilled cheese strips + soft fruit

🍽️ Dinner

  • Flaked salmon + mashed potato
  • Rice + beans + avocado
  • Soft meatball + pasta
  • Egg + sweet potato mash
  • Family meal adapted to a soft texture

🍌 Snacks

  • Banana + strawberry mash
  • Greek yogurt
  • Applesauce
  • Soft ripe pear or peach
  • Soft muffin pieces
Keep it low-pressure: These are idea banks, not a menu you have to follow. With a child who is still learning new textures, serve foods in a texture and size he can currently manage safely and introduce new textures gradually.
